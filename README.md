

# ⚡ Buzzword Dash

### 📚 Content
- **1,270 medical flashcards** across 15 subjects
- Subjects: Neurology, Cardiology, Nephrology, Psychiatry, Gastroenterology, Pulmonology, Infectious Disease, Endocrinology, Hematology/Oncology, Rheumatology, OB/GYN, Pediatrics, Surgery, Emergency Medicine, Multisystem
- **Adaptive card selection** — cards you miss appear more often (spaced repetition)
- **Teaching points** shown after wrong answers with "why wrong" explanations
- **Answer-leak detection** prevents buzzwords from revealing the answer
- **Custom card creator** — add your own flashcards with full validation
- **Import/Export** custom cards as JSON to share with classmates

### 🏃 Game Modes
- **Endless Run** — arcade mode, play until you run out of lives
- **Study Mode** — infinite lives, extended teaching points, no pressure
- **Weakness Mode** — targets your most-missed cards
- **Daily Round** — 15 cards, same for everyone each day, streak tracking

### 🎨 Visual Design
- **12 unique track skins** randomly selected each run (Neural Highway, Vascular Rush, Skeletal Corridor, Cellular Matrix, Neon ER, DNA Helix Tunnel, Prescription Sunset, Cardiac Pulse, Surgical Theater, Candy Lab, X-Ray Vision, Defibrillator Shock)
- **Scrolling ground markers** and **wall accent markers** for motion illusion
- **Dynamic skybox elements** — giant distant medical shapes (DNA helixes, pill capsules, hospital silhouettes) for deep parallax
- **Chibi-proportioned character** with big expressive eyes, visible smile, rounded limbs
- **Home screen character preview** with idle animations (breathing, clipboard check, stethoscope adjust, head tilt, wave)
- **Skin-specific ambient audio** — each track has unique drone tones
- **Running lights**, **atmospheric particles**, and **speed lines** that intensify with speed
- **Rush vignette** — orange screen-edge darkening during rush stacks

### 🏆 Progression
- **21 achievements** — First Steps, Perfect Run, streaks (10/25/50), scores (1K/5K/10K), coins (500/5K), encounters (100/500/1K), daily streaks (3/7/30), Renaissance Doc, Card Creator, Speed Demon, Shopper, Golden Doctor
- **6 daily quests** with coin rewards
- **Subject mastery badges** (⭐) when accuracy ≥ 80% with 50+ cards
- **Daily login rewards** that scale with consecutive days (10–200 coins)
- **Post-run review** with missed cards, teaching points, "why wrong" explanations, weakness identification, and priority review list

### 🎵 Audio
- **Procedural sound effects** — correct/wrong answer variations, coin collection, rush whoosh, power-up fanfare, achievement celebration
- **Streak pitch escalation** — each consecutive correct answer plays at a slightly higher pitch, creating an ascending musical scale that resets every 5 answers
- **Background music** with speed-reactive pitch shifting
- **Skin-specific ambient drones** — each track has unique tonal character
- **Text-to-Speech** — buzzwords read aloud with speed-adaptive rate
